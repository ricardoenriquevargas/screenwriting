# Markets, Funds and Submission Routes

Read this when the user asks how to get a script read, produced or funded. Programs, deadlines and rules change every year: when web search is available, check the official site for the current call before giving dates, amounts or requirements, and tell the user to confirm there.

## United States
- **Protect the work first**: U.S. Copyright Office registration (the strongest legal protection) and/or WGA Registry.
- **Representation**: most studios only accept scripts through agents, managers or entertainment lawyers. Managers are usually the first door for new writers.
- **Visibility routes**: screenwriting fellowships and labs (e.g. Academy Nicholl Fellowships, Sundance Institute labs, studio and network writing programs, many with diversity tracks), reputable competitions (e.g. Austin Film Festival), and script-hosting/evaluation services such as The Black List.
- **Independent route**: produce a proof-of-concept short, then pitch the feature with it.

## Spain
- Public funding through the national film agency (ICAA) and regional programs; television and streaming platforms co-finance many productions.
- Development and script programs at film schools, festivals and foundations; co-production markets at major festivals (e.g. San Sebastián, Málaga).
- Register the work with the Registro de la Propiedad Intelectual; collective management through the screenwriters' society.

## Latin America
- **Ibero-American co-production**: Programa Ibermedia supports development and co-production between member countries.
- **Peru**: the Ministry of Culture's audiovisual office (DAFO) runs yearly economic incentive contests (estímulos económicos) for screenplays, development, production and more. Register works with INDECOPI.
- **Mexico**: the national film institute (IMCINE) runs support programs for writing, development and production.
- **Colombia**: Proimágenes Colombia administers the Film Development Fund (FDC) and incentives for foreign shoots.
- **Chile, Argentina, Uruguay** and others have national film funds; Argentina's programs have changed significantly in recent years, so always check current status.
- **Markets and labs**: festivals with co-production forums and development labs across the region (e.g. Guadalajara, Cartagena, Ventana Sur, Bolivia Lab, Lima film festival industry sections).

## Streamers and studios
Platforms like Netflix, Prime Video, Max and Disney+ generally do not accept unsolicited scripts. The usual route is through a production company with an existing relationship, an agent or manager, or a market/lab where platform executives scout.

## Pitching etiquette
- Never send a script without permission; use a short query or one-pager first.
- Sign submission releases when required; keep records of who received what.
- Have the logline, one-pager and a clean PDF ready before reaching out.
- Follow up once after 3–4 weeks; then move on.
