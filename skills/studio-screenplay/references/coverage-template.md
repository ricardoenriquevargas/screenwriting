# Studio Coverage Template

Read this when the user asks for a review, notes or coverage of a script.

## Header
TITLE · Writer · Format/pages · Genre · Setting/period · Draft read · Date

## Logline
One or two sentences in the reader's words.

## Summary
Half a page to one page for a feature, covering the whole story including the ending.

## Comments
Write 1–2 pages of prose, strengths first, then problems in priority order. Cite page numbers and scenes.

## Verdict
- **Script**: Recommend / Consider / Pass
- **Writer**: Recommend / Consider / Pass (a writer can be worth following even if the script isn't there yet)

Explain the verdict in two or three sentences. Most professional scripts get "Consider" or "Pass"; reserve "Recommend" for scripts that are ready to go out.

## Note-giving principles
- Be specific: "On p. 34 Camila explains her backstory in a monologue; show it through the scar scene instead" beats "too much exposition".
- Diagnose the problem, suggest a direction, leave the solution to the writer.
- Separate taste from craft.
- End with the two or three highest-impact fixes for the next draft.
