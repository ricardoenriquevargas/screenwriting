# Screenwriting

**English** | [Español](#español)

Screenwriting helps Claude write film and TV screenplays in the format and with the materials that studios, production companies and streamers expect. It's built for screenwriters, actors, directors and producers, and it works in English, Spanish or any language you write in.

## What it does
- Guides you step by step: logline, synopsis, beat sheet, outline, script and rewrite.
- Uses industry-standard formatting: scene headings, action, dialogue and extensions.
- Delivers your script in Fountain format (opens in Final Draft, Highland, Fade In and WriterSolo) or as a PDF.
- Builds your pitch package: treatment, series bible, pitch deck and query letter.
- Gives studio-style coverage of your script: Recommend, Consider or Pass.

## How to use it
After adding it, ask Claude something like: "Help me write a feature about a female boxer" or "Give my script studio-style coverage."

## Data and privacy
This plugin contains text instructions only. It runs no code, connects to no external services, and sends or stores no data outside your conversation with Claude.

## Español

Screenwriting ayuda a Claude a escribir guiones de cine y series con el formato que piden los estudios, productoras y plataformas. Te guía por etapas (logline, sinopsis, beat sheet, escaleta, guion y reescritura), arma tu paquete de presentación y evalúa tu guion como un lector de estudio. Responde en español si le escribes en español.

Solo contiene instrucciones de texto: no ejecuta programas, no se conecta a servicios externos y no envía ni guarda datos fuera de tu conversación con Claude.
